# Leave Policy (fictional, for pipeline testing)

Applies to all full-time employees of Orbit Software Labs.

## Annual Leave

Each employee receives 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests longer than 3 consecutive days need manager approval at least 14 days in advance.

## Sick Leave

Each employee receives 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## Carry Forward

Up to 5 unused annual leave days may carry into the next year. Carried days must be used by 31 March.

## Parental Leave

Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Requests go to HR at least 60 days before the planned start date.
