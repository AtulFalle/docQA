# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Parties:** Orbit Software Labs and Globex Trading LLP
**Effective date:** 2025-01-01
**Expiry date:** 2026-12-31

This fictional agreement is provided for testing. The parties will exchange technical and commercial information to evaluate a possible data integration project.

## 1. Purpose

Each party may use the other's confidential information only to evaluate the proposed project.

## 2. Confidentiality Period

Confidentiality obligations last 3 years after each item is disclosed.

<!-- page: 2 -->

## 3. Permitted Disclosure

A recipient may share confidential information with employees and advisers who need to know, provided they are bound by equal obligations.

## 4. Return of Materials

On written request, the receiving party returns or destroys all confidential materials within 15 days.

## 5. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
