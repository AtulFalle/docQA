# Master Services Agreement

**Parties:** Orbit Software Labs ("Provider") and Acme Industries Pvt Ltd ("Client")
**Effective date:** 2025-02-16
**Expiry date:** 2027-02-15

This fictional agreement is provided for testing. It describes document processing and search services delivered under separate Statements of Work.

## 1. Services

Provider delivers document ingestion, search, and reporting services as described in each Statement of Work.

## 2. Fees and Payment

Client pays each invoice within 30 days of receipt. Late amounts accrue interest at 1 percent per month.

## 9. Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience with 90 days written notice.

## 10. Renewal

The agreement renews automatically for one-year terms unless either party gives written notice at least 60 days before the expiry date.

## 11. Limitation of Liability

Each party's total liability is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.

## 12. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the laws of Singapore.
